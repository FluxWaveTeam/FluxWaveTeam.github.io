Walor

Studio Head // 3D Animator // Lighting // Compositing // Editor
Transmission

Heyo, I'm Walor, an animator and a member of the FluxWave team. My role on this team involves animation and lighting, but my primary focus is lighting. I especially enjoy lighting and color correction, so I consider myself a lighting artist. I've been a part of the team since 2025.

I approach the creation of new projects seriously; I can work on both animation and lighting within the team. I'd also like to share my style, how I describe it, and what I strive for: Lighting - my job in lighting is to convey the emotions conveyed by the animation, so that people are immersed in the atmosphere I want to convey. My lighting in projects varies, depending on the mood/ambiance of the animation. Animation - my job in animation is to convey the emotions the character is experiencing, and more.

Fun fact: I love oatmeal cookies and lemon water.
